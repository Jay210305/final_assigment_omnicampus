1. The Business Problem Severe Leakage
  • 49.56% Observed Churn: 49,562 of 100,000 subscribers churned, eroding $2.87M/month ($34.45M/year) of a $5.85M/month revenue base.
  • Far Above Industry Norms: Big Three US postpaid churn averages 0.7%–1.1%/mo (~8%–13%/yr); replacement CAC runs $250–$600+ per subscriber.
  • Blanket Offers Fail: Offering $25 to all 100k users costs $2.50M, wastes $1.26M on non-churners, and yields only 37.4% 6-mo ROI.
2. Predictive ML Insight XGBoost 0.6981 AUC
  • Strong Non-Linear Signal: XGBoost achieves 0.6981 Test ROC-AUC (0.6864 PR-AUC), outperforming Logistic Regression (+5.7 pp) with zero CV-to-Test gap.
  • High Risk Concentration: Top Quintile (Q5) hits 74.00% churn (1.49x lift); Top Decile (D10) hits 78.50% (4.05x vs. D1).
  • Intervenable Drivers: 10 of top 15 features are actionable—led by handset age >365d (eqpdays_gt_365), device price, refurbished flag, ASL, and usage drops.
3. Two-Tier Retention Proposal 19,340 Subs (19.3%)
  • Tier 1A (4,175 subs @ $45): High-Value (rev_Mean >= $70.76) + Risk Q4/Q5 + Ageing Handset (>365d) → EIP upgrade credit + priority care.
  • Tier 1B (5,335 subs @ $25): High-Value + Risk Q4/Q5 + Newer Handset → Plan-rightsizing/ASL enrollment + priority care.
  • Tier 2 (9,830 subs @ $15): Mass-market Risk Q5 + intervenable trigger → 100% automated SMS/app self-service offer.
4. Financial Impact & Pilot Ask 90-Day A/B Test
  • 81.3% Lower Spend: $468,700 total budget ($24.23/user) protects $1.17M (6m) and $2.35M (12m) at a conservative 20% win-back rate.
  • Resilient Downside: Cash-flow positive at 6 months even at 10% win-back (+$117.9K); 7.99% 6-mo / 4.00% 12-mo break-even.
  • Decision Ask: Approve a 90-day 50/50 A/B pilot on 20% of the cohort (3,868 subs, $46,870 budget; Go threshold ≥8.0 pp churn reduction).

Validation Protocol
  • 80/20 Stratified Split: 80,000 train / 20,000 held-out test (random_state=42).

Why XGBoost Was Selected
  • Top Held-Out Generalization: Leads on Test ROC-AUC (0.6981), Test PR-AUC (0.6864), Test F1 (0.6451), and Test Accuracy (0.6438).

12-Week Execution Roadmap

Key Risks & Mitigation Matrix
Risk Category Specific Failure Mode Mitigation Strategy
1. Deadweight Loss Subsidizing false positives (31.4% of targeted cohort who would stay anyway). Already priced into all ROI/break-even math; further reduced via triggered delivery (Tier 1A EIP credit issued only upon engagement) and Q5 + behavioral gating.
2. ARPU Cannibalization Plan-rightsizing lowers recurring revenue from heavy overage payers (>$115/mo). Cap bill credits at 15% of monthly ARPU ($15–$25 one-time or 3-mo promo) instead of permanent rate-plan downgrades; track Net ARPU in A/B pilot.
3. Care Center Capacity Outbound Tier 1 calls overwhelm reps, causing >10-min waits or 20%–25% transfers. Route Tier 2 (50.8% of volume) to 100% automated SMS/app self-service, reserving live reps strictly for Tier 1 (~150 calls/day over 90 days).
4. Data & Seasonality 22%–49% demographic missingness, negative eqpdays, or Q4 flagship promo spikes. Model relies on >97% complete billing/usage fields with anomaly clipping; refresh XGBoost scores monthly and pre-stage Tier 1A offers 30 days before Q4 launches.
